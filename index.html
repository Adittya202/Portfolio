The Premise & Club Lore

Step into the official promotion trials of the BRAC University Computer Club (BUCC). Starting as a humble General Member (GM), players must run through campus hallways, dodge department peers, deflect high-velocity suppression rounds with a holographic energy shield, spend earned coins at the Promotion Weapon Store, and survive arena duels against real club executives to claim supreme leadership as Governing Body President!

5-Stage Promotional Gauntlet

Stage 1

GM to Executive — Classic Endless Runner Trials

"General Members are coming"

Play as a General Member (GM). Rightward auto-runner mechanics requiring timed jumps (Spacebar / ↑ / Click) to leap over 7 consecutive department enemies (C&M, Creative, Event Management, Finance, PR, HR, and R&D) with department banners displayed above their heads. Dodge all 7 to earn promotion.

Stage 2

Executive to Senior Executive — Suppression Gauntlet

"Executives are coming"

Promoted to Executive. 7 department executives storm the field firing high-velocity suppression rounds. Players must hold Right Click to deploy the Holographic Shield and deflect incoming projectile barrages. Surviving unlocks the Promotion Weapon Store.

Stage 3

Senior Executive Arena Duel — 14 Departmental Executives

"Senior executives are coming"

Ranked as Senior Executive. Transition into full 2D Arena Combat with omnidirectional aiming and tactical movement. Fight through 14 real BUCC Senior Executives (2 per department) with dynamic overhead name plates across all 7 departments:
• C&M: Md. Ishtiaq Mozumder, S.M.Abrar Shaleheen  |  Creative: MD. Mushfiqur Rahman, Mahajabin Islam
• Event Management: Fahim Faysal, Fahim Al Razy  |  Finance: Arnab, Raisa
• PR: Tanisha, Shovon Pr  |  HR: Adittya, Subrajit  |  R&D: Mahir Dyan, Siam Ferdous

Stage 4

Executive Board Trial — 7 EB Directors

"Executive Board Members are coming"

Earn promotion to Executive Board (EB). Confront the 7 Directorate leaders of BUCC entering in succession: Zawad Bhai (C&M), Luban Bhai (Creative), Rafi Bhai (Event Management), Rawnak Bhai (Finance), Kabya Apu (HR), Anika Apu (PR), and Abir Bhai (R&D). Defeating all 7 qualifies for Governing Body trials.

Stage 5

The Final Stand — Simultaneous 4-Boss Governing Body Battle

"All four Governing Body members will come together"

As Executive Board Champion, engage all 4 apex leaders fighting simultaneously with distinct bullet-hell mechanics. Eliminate all four to win the game and ascend to Governing Body President!

Technical Architecture & Engine Specifications

Procedural Canvas 2D

60 FPS requestAnimationFrame loop, custom AABB & radial collision algorithms, camera screen-shake, multi-layer parallax campus skyline, and real-time particle emitters.

Procedural Web Audio

Integrated Web Audio frequency oscillator generates all jump whooshes, laser beams, shield hits, coin chimes, and chiptune synth music with zero external audio dependencies required.

Dual Persistence

Lightweight PHP backend with CSRF token validation and session security for high scores, with automated client-side localStorage fallback for 100% serverless static preview.
